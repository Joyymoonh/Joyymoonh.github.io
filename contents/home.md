I am currently completing my undergraduate studies in **Artificial Intelligence** at **Xiamen University**, where I am a research assistant at the **Media Analytics and Computing Laboratory (MAC Lab)** with Prof. Rongrong Ji and Assoc. Prof. Jiayi Ji. I am also a research intern at the **College of Artificial Intelligence, Tsinghua University**, working with Prof. Yongchao Chen.

I am driven by one question: *what does a machine need in order to understand the physical world well enough to act in it reliably?* My answer so far is a shared, structured **representation of the world**, one that perception can build, language can query, and a planner can act on. I work on embodied intelligence and world models, and I am increasingly interested in **agentic robotics**: robots that plan, act, reflect on failures, and keep improving. My earlier work on 3D vision-language grounding, such as referring expression segmentation and tracking in point clouds and aerial-ground scenes, gave me the perceptual foundation for this direction.
